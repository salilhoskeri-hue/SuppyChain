      Section 05

      The SAP Primer

      The Dispatch Board and the Dossier describe what happens to freight and materials in the world. This is where it gets recorded — inside the system most large supply chains actually run on.

      37 T-CODES / 1 WALKTHROUGH

      SAP is not the only enterprise resource planning platform in use, but by transaction volume it is the largest by a wide margin — SAP itself states that 77% of the world's transaction revenue touches an SAP system at some point. For a supply chain professional, that makes SAP less of a "nice to know" and more of a lingua franca: the material master, the purchase order, and the goods receipt you'll work with at one company are structurally the same ones you'll work with at the next.

      Sourcing and Procurement is SAP's current name for the line of business that used to be called MM — Materials Management back in the SAP R/3 and ECC days. The rebrand under S/4HANA didn't change the transactional core; it folded in sourcing, contract management, and supplier collaboration around the same Procure-to-Pay backbone. It covers four things: the master data that identifies materials and vendors, the purchasing process that turns a need into an order, the inventory management that tracks what physically arrives, and the invoice verification that reconciles what was ordered against what's paid for — with every step posting straight into Finance (FI), and feeding Production Planning (PP), Sales (SD), and Warehouse Management on either side.

      77%of the world's transaction revenue touches an SAP system, per SAP's own figures
      MM → S/4Materials Management, SAP's module name since the early 1990s, is now branded Sourcing & Procurement
      3master data objects anchor the whole process: Material, Vendor, Purchasing Info Record

      Section 06

      SAP Ariba

      The SAP Primer covers the operational core — the PO, the goods receipt, the invoice. Ariba is the layer sitting in front of it: strategic sourcing, supplier collaboration, and the network connecting a buyer to millions of suppliers before any of that transactional machinery starts.

      SOURCING LAYER

      SAP Ariba is SAP's cloud suite for strategic sourcing, contract management, and supplier risk — the work that happens before a purchase order exists: running RFPs and auctions, negotiating and storing contracts, and vetting a supplier before they ever touch the S/4HANA procure-to-pay flow described above. The connectivity layer underneath it, historically called the Ariba Network, was folded into SAP Business Network in 2021; "Ariba" today refers to the application suite, while Business Network is the pipe millions of buyers and suppliers actually transact over.

      That network is genuinely large: SAP and its partners put it at over five million connected buyers and suppliers, more than 14 million users, and upward of $3 trillion in annual commerce flowing across roughly 190 countries — a figure SAP has grown steadily for over a decade and has repeatedly described as exceeding the combined commerce volume of major consumer marketplaces like eBay, Amazon, and Alibaba. For a buyer, that scale is the whole pitch: a supplier already onboarded to Ariba for one customer is usually one click from trading with the next.

      $3T+in annual commerce moves across SAP Business Network, per SAP's published figures
      5M+buyers and suppliers connected across roughly 190 countries
      40–80%of a typical company's Scope 3 emissions trace back to procurement decisions — Ariba's actual sustainability lever
